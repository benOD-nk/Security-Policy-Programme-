1. Purpose 
   This policy defines the requirements for managing access to Gallary Corp information systems,
   data, and facilities. It ensures that access is granted on the basis of business need and
   the principle of least privilege.

2. Scope 
   This policy applies to all employees, contractors, and third parties who access Gallary Corp
   systems or data.

3. Policy Statements
3.1 Access to systems and data must be granted based on the principle of least privilege.

3.2 All user accounts must be uniquely identifiable. Shared accounts are prohibited
    except where technically unavoidable and formally approved.

3.3 Multi-factor authentication (MFA) is mandatory for all remote access and all
    access to systems classified as Critical or High.

3.4 Access rights must be reviewed at minimum every 6 months and immediately upon
    a change in role or employment status.

3.5 Privileged access (administrator or root-level) must be separately provisioned
    and must not be used for day-to-day activities.

4. Roles and Responsibilities
   ROLES                                   RESPONSIBILITY 
   Information Security                 Own and maintain this policy. Conduct access reviews.  
   IT Operations                        Implement access controls per this policy and the standard.
   HR                                   Notify InfoSec of joiners, movers, and leavers within 24hrs. 
   Line Manager                         Approve access requests for their direct reports.
   All Staff                            Comply with this policy and report suspected violations.

   submitted to the Information Security team and approved in writing.
